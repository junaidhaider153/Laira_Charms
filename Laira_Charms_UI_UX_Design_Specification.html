🌸 Helpful Answers

Frequently Wondered Questions

Everything you need to know about polymer clay care, custom commission slots, and shipping.

Are clay charms waterproof and scratch-resistant? expand_more
Yes! While unfinished polymer clay can be porous, every Laira Charms piece is sealed with 3 glossy coats of jewelry-grade UV resin. They easily survive rain, sink splashes, and daily pocket rubbing. We do suggest avoiding perfume sprays or prolonged underwater soaking.
Can I request a custom character or pet portrait? expand_more
Absolutely! We open 15 bespoke pet and anime commission slots on the first Sunday of every month. You can submit reference photos through our Custom Atelier, and we will sculpt your favorite furry friend or gaming avatar with custom hardware.
How fast do charm orders ship out? expand_more
Ready-to-ship charms from our Weekly Drops ship within 24 to 48 hours. Custom Atelier pieces that require fresh baking, painting, and UV glazing cure in 3 to 5 business days before departing our sweet studio.
What is your 30-Day Snag & Snap Warranty? expand_more
If your charm ever snaps from its jump ring or sustains damage within 30 days of arrival during normal daily wear, send us a quick photo! We will fix it at no cost or craft an identical replacement right away.
